## Decision boundaries

- Stage A does not authorize Stages B–F.
- A scientific reviewer signature cannot substitute for a provider contract, IRB/HRPP determination, disclosure partner, deployment authority, author approval, or journal decision.
- An accountable-human or external-authority signature cannot substitute for the independent scientific review required by the schema-v4 phase gate.
- When a jurisdiction, institution, provider, venue, partner, or data source is unnamed, the disposition is `WAIT_EXTERNAL`, not inferred approval.
- No private key, credential, identity document, unpublished exploit detail, participant/customer data, or proprietary source code may enter public-web processing.

## Official guidance used for routing

- HHS OHRP explains that institutional HRPP/IRB offices commonly determine whether an activity constitutes non-exempt human-subjects research and that interaction, intervention, or identifiable private information can trigger the framework: [What Is Human Subjects Research?](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/education-and-outreach/online-education/human-research-protection-training/lesson-2-what-is-human-subjects-research/index.html) and [Coded Private Information Guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/ohrp/coded-private-information-or-biospecimens-used-research.html).
- CISA describes coordinated vulnerability disclosure as a managed triage, remediation, and disclosure process, supporting a named process owner before private-vulnerability work: [CISA Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Program](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/programs/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure-program) and [CISA/NSA CVD Guidance](https://www.cisa.gov/resources-tools/resources/establishing-coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure-program-work-security-researchers).
- ICMJE assigns corresponding authors responsibility for journal communication and completion of authorship, ethics, registration, and disclosure requirements, while requiring all authors to approve the final manuscript: [Defining the Role of Authors and Contributors](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/roles-and-responsibilities/defining-the-role-of-authors-and-contributors.html) and [Submission and Peer-Review Responsibilities](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/roles-and-responsibilities/responsibilities-in-the-submission-and-peer-peview-process.html).

These sources guide routing only. The governing jurisdiction, institution, contracts, target venue, and accountable experts must determine the actual requirements.
