## Solution contract

ChronosAudit is viable only if a versioned information-admissibility protocol can
produce auditable real-case manifests, joint independence assignments, and
censor-aware outcome states before detector performance is calculated. The
method must then show how apparent performance changes across the prespecified
split ladder and must quantify selective prediction and analyst workload under
realistic base rates. A sealed prospective extension is a separate later-stage
claim and is not part of the currently authorized solution.

## Failure envelope

The full solution is defeated or must be narrowed if any of the following holds:

1. representative real incidents cannot be reconstructed with lawful,
   prediction-time source or bytecode provenance;
2. lineage, clone, proxy, mechanism, or outcome assignments change eligibility
   under independent rerun after prespecified refinement;
3. no sufficiently strong public baseline portfolio can be reproduced fairly;
4. the joint controls do not materially change the capability estimate or its
   uncertainty;
5. base-rate and analyst-workload evaluation cannot be grounded in a defined
   eligible population; or
6. the prospective extension lacks independent governance or informative event
   yield.
