## Claim boundary

ChronosAudit does **not** claim novelty for real smart-contract incidents,
historical chain state, executable grading, post-cutoff evaluation, clone
detection, exploit taxonomies, censoring, selective prediction, calibration,
base-rate analysis, analyst workload, or prospective scanning individually.

The only claim eligible for novelty assurance is:

> `C001-JOINT`: within a documented public search scope, ChronosAudit may be
> materially differentiating as an auditable measurement protocol that tests
> the interaction of prediction-time information admissibility; joint temporal,
> protocol/entity-lineage, source/bytecode/proxy/attacker-clone and exploit-
> mechanism separation; exploit-specific open-population status; a
> prespecified capability-survival ladder; calibrated workload-aware selective
> prediction; and a longitudinal sealed prospective cohort with frozen inputs,
> thresholds, follow-up, and independent adjudication.

This is a hypothesis about a joint protocol and its measurable consequences.
It is not a universal first-of-kind claim and is defeated if the integration is
only an obvious checklist of known components without a nonredundant,
machine-verifiable interaction or decision consequence.

## Strongest composite objection

Even if no single predecessor implements the full bundle, the contribution may
be an obvious recombination of historical/executable smart-contract
benchmarks, post-cutoff scaffold evaluation, clone-overlap auditing, Web3
survival analysis, selective classification, exploit prioritization, standards,
and one-time recent-contract security-agent studies. ChronosAudit survives this
objection only if its joint admissibility graph is independently rerunnable and
the interaction of controls yields a scientifically identifiable capability-
survival result that none of the components can supply alone.

## Attempt-8 authorized superseding claim

The broader `C001-JOINT` hypothesis and its attempt-5/attempt-7 search history
remain preserved above. For attempt 8 only, the user authorized the following
narrower claim before the exact-match audit:

> `C001-MEASUREMENT`: A preregistered measurement study testing how much
> apparent exploit-detection capability survives the joint application of
> temporal, lineage, clone-family, mechanism-family, censoring, and
> operational-workload controls.

For operational screening, the control terms mean:

- temporal admissibility at the prediction time;
- protocol and entity lineage separation;
- normalized source, bytecode, proxy-implementation, and attacker-contract
  clone-family separation;
- exploit-mechanism-family separation;
- an open-population outcome model that distinguishes confirmed positives,
  mature investigated negatives, and unresolved right-censored contracts; and
- selective-prediction and analyst-workload consequences under prespecified
  base rates and alert budgets.

The novelty object is the capability-survival measurement estimand: the
prespecified change in apparent capability, uncertainty, coverage, and workload
as the controls are applied jointly or through a frozen split ladder. The
prospective shadow cohort is a later validation surface and is not part of the
attempt-8 novelty claim.

## Attempt-8 materiality and defeating rules

The claim is materially differentiating only if the joint measurement answers
a scientific or operational question that isolated leakage checks cannot answer.
A predecessor defeats the claim if it prespecifies and measures an equivalent
multi-axis capability-survival or performance-degradation estimand spanning
temporal control plus lineage or clone dependence plus mechanism, censoring, or
workload consequences, and reports their interaction or decision consequence.
It need not use the ChronosAudit name, identical software, or every proposed
manifest field.

- `NOVELTY_SURVIVES`: no defeating predecessor is recovered in the bounded
  audit, known-item checks pass, and the independent challenger finds the
  capability-survival estimand materially nonredundant. This is evidence-bounded,
  not universal novelty proof.
- `NOVELTY_UNRESOLVED`: exact-match retrieval, known-item recovery, or closest-
  predecessor traversal is incomplete or materially ambiguous.
- `REFRAME`: the remaining difference is implementation, packaging, scale,
  domain transfer, or replication rather than the measurement estimand.
- `STOP`: an equivalent joint capability-survival measurement study is verified.
