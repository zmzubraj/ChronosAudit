# ChronosAudit Candidate Portfolio

## Scope and status

- Claim ID: `C001`
- Phase: `NOVELTY_AUDIT`
- Inputs used: `problem-investigation.md`, `causal-model.mmd`, `search-protocol.md`, `evidence-ledger.csv`
- Status boundary: this portfolio is a falsification-first synthesis artifact. It does not advance novelty, feasibility, or program state.

## Synthesis answer

The search evidence collapses any broad "first real-incident smart-contract benchmark" story. Attempt 2 also collapses component claims for right-censored Web3 benchmarking and a one-time prospective recent-contract zero-day scan: FinSurvival already operationalizes censoring in temporal Web3 benchmarks, while SCONE scanned 2,849 recently deployed contracts, found previously unknown vulnerabilities in simulation, reported cost and revenue, and coordinated disclosure or rescue. Attempt 3 further collapses component claims for operational pre-outcome exploit prioritization, proxy-aware verification checklists, attacker-contract analysis, exploit reuse, campaign structure, and execution-trace clustering through FIRST EPSS, OWASP SCSVS, EEA EthTrust, BlockWatchdog, and *Evil Under the Sun* (`PA031`-`PA035`).

The only supportable differentiator still on the table is a *joint interaction and measurement protocol* claim: ChronosAudit may remain scientifically interesting if it can prove that prediction-time admissibility, lineage and clone controls, mechanism separation, exploit-specific open-population status, workload-aware selective prediction, and a longitudinal sealed prospective cohort jointly change the apparent capability story in ways that existing benchmarks do not already show.

That claim is still fragile. Every major ingredient already has a strong predecessor. ChronosAudit therefore only survives as a candidate if it is framed as a contamination-audit and capability-survival method, not as a detector advance and not as a generic exploit benchmark.

## Candidate families

### Family A: retrospective split-ladder benchmark

- Objective: estimate performance decay as controls are added from weak splits to strict retrospective admissibility.
- Evidence basis: `PA011`, `PA014`, `PA013`, `PA010`, `PA012`, `PA031`, `PA032`, `PA034`, `PA035`
- Strength:
  - lowest operational and governance burden;
  - publishable even if the strictest controls cause severe performance collapse;
  - directly tests the contamination hypothesis.
- Hard gates:
  - a rerunnable admissibility manifest must exist at case and prediction-time level;
  - lineage, clone, and mechanism family assignments must be reproducible enough for audit;
  - the split ladder must be frozen before confirmatory analysis.
- Main uncertainty:
  - retrospective controls may still be challenged as insufficiently operational without an external or prospective phase.
- Negative-result value:
  - high. A strong negative finding still establishes whether reported exploit-detection capability survives contamination control.

### Family B: censor-aware historical surveillance benchmark

- Objective: treat contracts as an evolving risk population with confirmed positives, mature negatives where justified, and unresolved right-censored observations.
- Evidence basis: `PA008`, `PA022`, `PA023`, `PA024`, `PA025`, `PA026`, `PA027`, `PA033`
- Strength:
  - best alignment with the open-world nature of pre-incident exploit discovery;
  - directly addresses the unrealistic-negative problem;
  - supplies an exploit-specific application of established survival methodology, rather than methods novelty.
- Hard gates:
  - maturity rules for negatives must be explicit, auditable, and defensible;
  - censor-aware estimands must remain interpretable to security and benchmark audiences;
  - follow-up quality must be sufficient to avoid arbitrary status assignment.
- Main uncertainty:
  - FinSurvival already defeats right-censoring novelty in Web3; only the exploit-outcome and joint-admissibility binding remains open.
- Negative-result value:
  - high. Even if detector performance is weak, showing that naive closed-world labeling materially misstates security utility is a publishable methodological result.

### Family C: hybrid benchmark plus sealed prospective shadow deployment

- Objective: combine the retrospective ladder with a frozen forward cohort to test sustained precision-coverage-workload performance under a sealed protocol.
- Evidence basis: `PA011`, `PA012`, `PA013`, `PA008`, `PA022`, `PA026`, `PA027`, `PA033`
- Strength:
  - strongest answer to the user-stated operational question if it is longitudinal and more tightly sealed than SCONE;
  - best match to the target contribution if the cohort, thresholds, retrieval process, and follow-up remain genuinely frozen;
  - cleanest way to distinguish a one-time zero-day demonstration from sustained operational usefulness.
- Hard gates:
  - preregistered longitudinal cohort and follow-up window, plus frozen model, retrieval surface, thresholds, adjudication rules, and disclosure workflow;
  - enough follow-up time and incident yield to support a meaningful decision;
  - independent adjudication and safe handling of any actionable findings.
- Main uncertainty:
  - SCONE already defeats novelty of a one-time prospective scan, so the longitudinal freeze and independent adjudication must be material rather than cosmetic;
  - the prospective layer remains easy to invalidate through leakage, drift, or low event volume.
- Negative-result value:
  - moderate to high. A prospective null result is still valuable if the protocol is clean, because it bounds current real-world utility rather than merely historical fit.

## Hard-gate comparison

| Family | What it can prove | What defeats it | Lowest defensible outcome if the detector is weak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Whether apparent capability survives increasingly strict retrospective contamination controls. | Non-rerunnable admissibility rules, unstable family assignments, or unresolved-label distortion. | Benchmark-science paper showing contamination-driven performance collapse. |
| B | Whether open-world, unresolved populations materially change measured utility. | Undefendable maturity rules or censoring assumptions. | Method paper showing that binary closed-world evaluation is misleading. |
| C | Whether a frozen system sustains useful alert quality under real base-rate and workload conditions. | Any break in the seal, weak adjudication, or too little adjudicable signal. | Operational null result with a credible upper bound on current deployable utility. |

## Recommended ordering

1. Treat **Family A + Family B** as the minimum benchmark-science core.
2. Treat **Family C** as a contingent extension that upgrades the operational claim only if the seal, adjudication, and follow-up windows are credible.
3. Frame all three families around the same central estimand: *capability survival under contamination control*, not exploit prediction as an already-solved problem.

## Rejected or downgraded alternatives

### Detector-first novelty story

- Rejected because `PA005`, `PA006`, `PA010`, `PA011`, `PA012`, and `PA013` already make the detector-and-benchmark surface crowded.
- ChronosAudit does not survive as a detector paper on the present evidence.

### Time-only or post-cutoff novelty story

- Rejected because `PA011` and `PA012` already defeat it.
- Time control is necessary but not differentiating.

### Clone-control-only novelty story

- Rejected because `PA014`, `PA015`, `PA016`, and `PA017` already defeat it.
- Clone control matters only as part of the broader admissibility protocol.

### "First real-incident executable smart-contract benchmark" story

- Rejected because `PA013`, `PA012`, and `PA010` already defeat it.

### Right-censored Web3 benchmark novelty story

- Rejected because `PA026` and `PA027` already implement large-scale censored temporal Web3 benchmarks.
- ChronosAudit can only claim an exploit-specific application whose value is demonstrated under joint admissibility control.

### One-time prospective zero-day scan novelty story

- Rejected because `PA012` already reports a recent-contract simulation over 2,849 contracts, two previously unknown vulnerabilities, operating cost, simulated revenue, and disclosure or rescue coordination.
- ChronosAudit must specify a longitudinal sealed cohort with frozen thresholds, preserved abstentions, follow-up, and independent adjudication.

### Attacker-contract, campaign, or exploit-reuse novelty story

- Rejected because `PA034` directly analyzes attacker contracts and cross-contract call chains, while `PA035` documents exploit-contract reuse, cross-DApp propagation, campaign structure, and trace-based clustering.
- Attacker and campaign families remain valuable as leakage controls, but recognizing those dependencies is not novel.

### Operational exploit-probability or prioritization novelty story

- Rejected because `PA033` operationalizes daily, time-indexed exploit probabilities, future-outcome evaluation, realistic base rates, and prioritization trade-offs for published CVEs.
- ChronosAudit's remaining question is smart-contract-specific capability survival after joint admissibility control, not the invention of pre-outcome exploit scoring or workload prioritization.

### Proxy-aware or tiered verification-standard novelty story

- Rejected because `PA031` and `PA032` provide public smart-contract verification controls and tiered audit-assurance requirements.
- Standards may inform the case manifest and evidence ladder, but cannot be presented as a ChronosAudit invention.

## Decisive tests for the next phase

### T1. Joint-bundle defeat test

- Question: after independent citation challenge, does any single predecessor already combine most of the ChronosAudit bundle closely enough that the remaining differences are cosmetic?
- Pass condition: no such predecessor is found and the material differentiator remains the combined protocol.

### T1b. SCONE incremental-value test

- Question: after treating SCONE as the closest prospective predecessor, does ChronosAudit's longitudinal freeze, joint independence policy, censor-aware population, and calibrated workload produce a scientifically different estimand rather than merely a larger scan?
- Pass condition: the incremental protocol is prespecified, auditable, and capable of changing the capability conclusion or its uncertainty.

### T2. Lineage-control test

- Question: can protocol/entity/factory/fork lineage be defined with enough reproducibility that split assignments are auditable rather than subjective?
- Pass condition: lineage assignment rules can be frozen and independently rerun.

### T3. Censor-aware population test

- Question: can mature-negative versus unresolved states be operationalized without turning the benchmark into arbitrary analyst judgment?
- Pass condition: a defensible maturity rule and escalation path exist before confirmatory analysis.

### T4. Prospective seal test

- Question: can a prospective cohort be frozen tightly enough that any positive prospective result would remain interpretable?
- Pass condition: model, retrieval, thresholds, cohort, adjudication, and disclosure boundaries can all be frozen and audited.

### T5. Negative-result usefulness test

- Question: if strict-control performance collapses, is the result still scientifically valuable?
- Pass condition: yes, provided the collapse can be attributed to contamination control and not to uncontrolled data-quality failure.

## Residual risks and objections

- A broader patent-family or inaccessible proprietary-deployment record could still surface a closer predecessor; the public surveillance, standards, exploit-prioritization, and attacker-campaign vocabularies were materially expanded in attempt 3.
- The maturity-rule and right-censoring layer may prove statistically defensible in theory but operationally too subjective for a benchmark audience.
- If the prospective layer cannot exceed SCONE with a documented longitudinal seal and independent adjudication, ChronosAudit should contract to a retrospective benchmark-science paper rather than preserve an operational-utility claim by rhetoric.
- Because every ingredient already has precedent, weak implementation discipline will collapse the project into a recombination paper with little novelty headroom.
