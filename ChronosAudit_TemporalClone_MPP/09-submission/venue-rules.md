# Dated venue rules

Checked: 2026-08-29. Official venue instructions override this local summary.

## Immediate dissemination: arXiv

Official sources:

- Submission overview: https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit/index.html
- TeX submission guidance: https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_tex.html

Applicable gates:

- A registered author submits their own work and may require endorsement.
- The submission must be topical and refereeable; moderation is not peer review.
- The submitter chooses a distribution license and must hold the necessary rights.
- TeX source and all required figures/files must be included; arXiv compiles the source.
- The accountable author must inspect the generated preview before final submission.

Local disposition: a flat TeX source bundle is prepared, but author identity, license choice, downstream rights, independent reproduction, and human preview/approval are unresolved.

## Preferred peer-reviewed target: Empirical Software Engineering

Official source: https://link.springer.com/journal/10664/submission-guidelines

Applicable gates recorded from the current guide:

- Original Paper route; manuscript normally uses 150--250 word abstract and 4--6 keywords.
- Title page must include author names, affiliations, corresponding-author contact, and available ORCIDs.
- Statements and declarations must address funding, competing interests, author contributions, and data availability as applicable.
- LaTeX source is accepted; submission files should avoid unsupported folder structures.
- Human authors remain accountable for content; generative systems cannot be authors.

Local disposition: the content architecture matches a regular empirical paper, but official Springer template/portal validation and all accountable-author fields remain to be completed.

## Binding decision

The immediate local bundle is a **submission-ready draft**, not an authorized submission. After the open gates close, use arXiv for rapid public dissemination and submit the same evidence-bounded manuscript to Empirical Software Engineering or a freshly re-verified alternative.
