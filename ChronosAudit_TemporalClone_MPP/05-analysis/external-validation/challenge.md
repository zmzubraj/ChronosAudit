# External-validation challenge

The strongest objection is that a benchmark-specific audit could be narrated as a
general smart-contract ecosystem result. The manuscript blocks that inference in its
title, estimands, limitations, captions, data-availability statement, and conclusion.
The second objection is that exact bytecode groups could be called semantic clones;
the manuscript calls them fingerprint families only. The third is that 67 proxy
indicators could be called a prevalence estimate; the manuscript calls it a lower-bound
observable count within SCONE-bench. These protections must be checked again by an
independent reviewer before submission.
