# Preregistration and Deviations

No public preregistration preceded the original data acquisition. The present MPP is a retrospective secondary analysis. The analysis contract was frozen locally before the final derivation used in the manuscript.

## Known pre-freeze exploration

Informal exploratory counts were inspected before this protocol. They suggested the selected landmark, bytecode, and proxy summaries. Therefore, the analysis must not be described as prospectively preregistered or confirmatory in the strict sense.

## Recorded correction

An initial implementation mistakenly used `cutoff_lead_hours` for landmark eligibility. A failing integration test exposed the mismatch. The protocol was corrected so eligibility uses deployment-to-incident age—the quantity that answers whether the contract existed at the landmark. The cutoff lead remains a separate descriptive variable. The correction occurred before manuscript result freeze and is preserved in the test history.

## Future deviations

Any new identity abstraction, subgroup, correlation, hypothesis test, or endpoint introduced after this freeze must be labeled exploratory and added here with rationale and affected claims.
