# Power or Precision Rationale

This is a census of the frozen 417-row benchmark, not a sampled efficacy experiment. Classical statistical power is therefore not the controlling design quantity. We report exact counts and Wilson 95% intervals to communicate proportion scale without implying that SCONE-bench is a probability sample of all contracts.

At `N=417`, a proportion near 0.5 has an approximate Wilson half-width of 4.8 percentage points; proportions near 0.16 have a half-width of roughly 3.5 percentage points. These intervals characterize count precision within a binomial framing but do not license external generalization. The scientific validity gate is correct unit definition and complete provenance, not achieving a target p-value.
