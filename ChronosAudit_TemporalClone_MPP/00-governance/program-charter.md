- System: ChronosAudit TemporalClone MPP

- Target venue/article type: venue-neutral empirical measurement article plus arXiv preprint; peer-reviewed target pending dated venue-fit and fee decision

- External-processing permission: bibliographic metadata and public-source verification only; no raw case envelopes or unpublished result files may be uploaded

## User-asserted starting contract

These items are intake assertions (`E1 ASSERTED`), not verified novelty, feasibility, or evidence.

- Core research question: Among the 417 counter-authorized historical exploit cases, how do prespecified pre-incident temporal landmarks and progressively stronger contract-identity abstractions (chain-address, exact runtime bytecode, metadata-stripped runtime bytecode, and observable proxy linkage) change usable-case coverage and measured benchmark dependence?
- Novelty statement: The asserted material difference is a reproducible benchmark-validity audit that jointly binds outcome-independent historical cutoffs, dual-provider EIP-1898 state reconstruction, landmark-specific eligibility, and cross-address clone/proxy dependency analysis; neither historical execution nor smart-contract clone detection alone is claimed as novel.
- Target contribution: A minimum publishable measurement and artifact contribution comprising a frozen 417-case manifest, deterministic analysis pipeline, editable result tables, quantitative figures, reviewed algorithms and diagrams, an evidence-bounded manuscript, and a reproducible submission package; it explicitly excludes exploit prediction, matched-control effectiveness, causal risk estimation, production readiness, and R0-R5 detector claims.
- Possible feasibility: Existing local evidence includes 417 counter-authorized strict historical snapshots with a 20,000-deployment denominator available only as contextual provenance, dual-provider observations, cutoff/deployment/incident times, runtime bytecode, metadata-stripped bytecode, and proxy indicators. The MPP will reuse these read-only artifacts without new RPC collection, recruitment, intervention, or external sharing. A focused 7-10 working-day implementation and writing sprint is plausible, subject to frozen-result reproduction, prior-art challenge, licensing and data-sharing review, qualified human scientific review, current venue verification, and final human submission approval.
- External or real-world validation requirement: N/A for the narrow descriptive audit; claims are limited to the frozen SCONE-bench cohort and do not generalize to all smart contracts or benchmark performance
- Exploratory versus confirmatory analysis boundary: prior informal counts were exploratory; the predeclared v1 derivation and frozen 417-file manifest define the primary reproducible analysis, with all later additions labeled exploratory or deviations
- Feasibility-pilot status and authority: PILOT NOT REQUIRED for read-only reuse; deterministic full-cohort derivation is the cheapest decisive feasibility test
- Solution-viability status: ASSERTED ONLY; claim-matched evidence is required
- Acceptance-readiness status: NOT ASSESSABLE; editorial decisions remain external
