Name of this system: ChronosAudit TemporalClone MPP
Research topic name: Outcome-independent historical on-chain state reconstruction, temporal landmark eligibility, and clone/proxy linkage auditing in a benchmark of 417 real-world EVM smart-contract exploit incidents across Ethereum, BSC, Base, and Arbitrum.
Core research question: Among the 417 counter-authorized historical exploit cases, how do prespecified pre-incident temporal landmarks and progressively stronger contract-identity abstractions (chain-address, exact runtime bytecode, metadata-stripped runtime bytecode, and observable proxy linkage) change usable-case coverage and measured benchmark dependence?
Novelty statement: The asserted material difference is a reproducible benchmark-validity audit that jointly binds outcome-independent historical cutoffs, dual-provider EIP-1898 state reconstruction, landmark-specific eligibility, and cross-address clone/proxy dependency analysis; neither historical execution nor smart-contract clone detection alone is claimed as novel.
The target contribution should be: A minimum publishable measurement and artifact contribution comprising a frozen 417-case manifest, deterministic analysis pipeline, editable result tables, quantitative figures, reviewed algorithms and diagrams, an evidence-bounded manuscript, and a reproducible submission package; it explicitly excludes exploit prediction, matched-control effectiveness, causal risk estimation, production readiness, and R0-R5 detector claims.
Possible feasibility: Existing local evidence includes 417 counter-authorized strict historical snapshots with a 20,000-deployment denominator available only as contextual provenance, dual-provider observations, cutoff/deployment/incident times, runtime bytecode, metadata-stripped bytecode, and proxy indicators. The MPP will reuse these read-only artifacts without new RPC collection, recruitment, intervention, or external sharing. A focused 7-10 working-day implementation and writing sprint is plausible, subject to frozen-result reproduction, prior-art challenge, licensing and data-sharing review, qualified human scientific review, current venue verification, and final human submission approval.
